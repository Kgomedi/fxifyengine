Intent

Target traders researching how to pass prop firm evaluations. High commercial intent — close to purchasing. Rank for "fxify evaluation tips" and convert to sign-ups.

The evaluation isn't hard because the markets are unpredictable. It's hard because most traders ignore the one thing that matters most — risk management. You can have a solid strategy and still blow your evaluation in two bad trades.

Know Your Numbers Before You Trade

Before you place a single trade, know your daily drawdown limit, your max drawdown, and your profit target cold. These aren't suggestions — they're the rules of the game. Treat them like a stop loss on the whole account.

Size Your Positions Properly

Most traders fail by over-leveraging when they're down. If you're at 60% of your daily limit, the answer is never to double your position. Drop your size in half and protect what you have left.

Trade Your Best Setups Only

The evaluation window creates pressure. You feel like you need to trade every day. You don't. Waiting two or three days for a clean setup will always serve you better than forcing mediocre ones.
